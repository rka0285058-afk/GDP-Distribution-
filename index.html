TrendScenario

Method: each year an optimiser picks sector growth rates and employment shifts that hit the GDP target exactly while narrowing earnings growth gaps across demographic groups, states and deciles. The inclusion push sets how strongly gaps are penalised against distance from trend growth. Investment cost is extra capital for sectors pushed above trend (ICOR times extra output). Costs are gross, in constant FY2027 rupees, and ignore capital freed where other sectors slow. Scenarios are precomputed on a grid of targets and pushes; cost assumptions recalculate live.
